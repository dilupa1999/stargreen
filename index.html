The Ather 450X offers an Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) range of up to 161 km for the 3.7 kWh battery variant and 126 km for the 2.9 kWh variant. Actual range may vary based on riding mode, terrain, and individual riding habits. True Range for Ather 450X 3.7 kWh battery variant is 130 km and 105 km for 2.9 kWh variant.

The Ather 450X electric scooter is available starting at Rs. 924,900. Visit your nearest showroom or call +94 77 755 3355 for more details.

All Ather scooters are available for purchase in Sri Lanka at the flagship store in Maradana or through authorized dealers islandwide.

Riding an Ather 450X is pure joy. With its low centre of gravity and sturdy aluminium frame chassis, it stands out as one of the best electric scooters on the road. Accelerating from 0 to 40 km/h in just 3.3 seconds and reaching a top speed of 90 km/h, it’s built for both quick city rides and smooth journeys on any road in Sri Lanka. Supported by Ather’s expanding fast-charging grid across the country, the 450X offers a range of up to 161 km on a single charge, giving you the freedom to travel farther without worry. Don’t just take our word for it — book a test ride at our flagship store in Maradana or through authorized dealers islandwide and experience the difference yourself.

The scooter is capable of reaching a top speed of 90 km/h, providing ample power for city commuting.

Yes, the 17.7 cm (7”) TFT touchscreen dashboard features integrated Google Maps for turn-by-turn navigation. You can also manage music playback and incoming calls directly from the dash, keeping your phone safely in your pocket.

The scooter includes a Fall Safe feature that cuts off motor power in the event of a fall, an Emergency Stop Signal (ESS) that flashes the taillight during panic braking, and Park Assist, which helps you reverse out of tight spots effortlessly.

AutoHold is a smart safety feature that automatically prevents your scooter from rolling back or forward on inclines and declines. Once you come to a complete stop on a slope, the system uses the motor and onboard sensors to hold the vehicle steady no need to keep the brakes pressed. It releases instantly as soon as you twist the throttle to move.

The Ather Rizta is designed for comfort and utility. It features the largest seat in its category, providing ample room for a rider and pillion. It also offers massive storage capacity up to 56 liters (34L under-seat + 22L optional "Frunk") and focuses on a smoother, more relaxed ride with a top speed of 80 km/h.

Electric power is significantly more cost-effective. Riding an Ather 450X can reduce your energy costs per kilometer by a wide margin compared to a 125-150cc petrol scooter, allowing for substantial annual savings depending on your daily usage.
