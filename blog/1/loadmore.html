The Ather Rizta S: Redefining the Sri Lankan Family Ride

📅 May 13, 2026

In Sri Lanka, a scooter is rarely just for one person. It’s the family’s daily workhorse. Whether it’s the school run, a quick trip to the grocery store, or a weekend ride with a partner, comfort and space are non-negotiable. Enter the Ather Rizta S, a scooter specifically engineered to put the "family" back in commuting.

Space for Everyone and Everything
The first thing you’ll notice about the Rizta S is its XL seat. At 900mm, it is the longest in its category, ensuring that two adults can ride comfortably without feeling cramped. But the space doesn't stop at the seat. The Rizta S solves the "where do I put this?" problem with a massive 34L under-seat storage capacity - large enough for two full-face helmets or a week’s worth of groceries. For even more utility, the optional 22L "Frunk" (front trunk) brings your total storage to a staggering 56 liters.

Built for Stability and Safety
Riding with a pillion passenger or heavy bags requires extra stability. The Rizta S features a low center of gravity and a wide floorboard, making it incredibly easy to balance even at low speeds in Colombo traffic. The Smart Choice With a Range of 123 km and a top speed of 80 km/h, the Rizta S is the perfect blend of efficiency and performance. It’s not just an electric scooter; it’s a comfortable, spacious, and safe upgrade that your entire family deserves.
